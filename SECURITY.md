# Security policy

This policy applies to every Ariesworx repository that does not publish its own.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not open a public issue, pull request or discussion.**

Report it privately on the affected repository: open its **Security** tab and
choose **Report a vulnerability**. Only the maintainers can see the report.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with
it. A person reads every report. We will acknowledge it, keep you informed while
we work on a fix, and credit you in the published advisory unless you would
rather we didn't.

## Supported versions

Unless a repository says otherwise, fixes go into its latest release or its
`main` branch only.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue or support legal action against research done in good faith
that respects this policy: report promptly, give us reasonable time to fix
before disclosing, and avoid privacy violations, data destruction and service
disruption. Test only against systems you own or are allowed to test.
